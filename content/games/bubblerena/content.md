## Overview

A first-person arena shooter crossed with match-3, made solo in 48 hours for the theme "Bubble". You run, jump and dash around an arena shooting colored bubbles to make matches of three, with waves that grow each round and a combo meter that turns popped bubbles into a shield around you.
