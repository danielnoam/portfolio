## Overview

A platformer made solo in my second semester to learn movement feel and level tooling. Wall jumps, dashes, coyote time, jump buffering and variable jump height, across levels with soft, crumbling and moving platforms, portals and secrets.



</div>

<div class="project-card">

## Editor tools

Player stats and abilities are tunable assets. A jump measurement tool shows reach in the scene, camera zones are edited with a trigger tool, and platform paths draw themselves in the editor.
